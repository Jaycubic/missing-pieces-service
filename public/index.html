How to Play

The Missing Pieces is a cooperative deduction game for four players. Together, you'll piece together the story behind a small mixed-up workplace mystery — some information is visible only to you, some only to your teammates, and one piece of your own story is hidden even from yourself.

Discover

Each of you starts with two visible clues and one hidden one. You can't see your own hidden clue, but everyone else can. Ask a teammate for a hint, or take your best guess whenever you feel ready.

Connect

Once everyone's caught up, work together to spot which clues on the table are meaningfully linked across the group.

Rule Book

Players — Four, each on their own device, in the same live session.

Goals — Piece together the full story: solve your own hidden clue, and find every meaningful connection.

Rules — Asking for a hint costs one of your three tokens. Guess your hidden clue any time. Once caught up, propose connections between any two clues.

Space — Everyone plays from their own device, in the same shared session.

Time — Two short timed rounds, a couple of minutes each.

Resources — Three hint tokens per player, and a fixed set of clue categories.

Conflict — Limited tokens mean choosing carefully who to ask and when, racing the clock as a team.
